## FRED / ALFRED finding and source change

[FRED terms](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/legal/) (undated, inspected 10 November 2026) contain restrictions concerning software/AI use and storage. The exploratory FRED download was withdrawn, its payload removed, and the FX pipeline replaced with the original Federal Reserve XML. A metadata-only removal record is in `reports/market_source_withdrawal.json`; Lebanese observations were unchanged. FRED is not an active data provider here. [API keys](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/api_key.html) require a registered account; a key alone would not resolve use restrictions.

[ALFRED documentation](https://alfred.stlouisfed.org/help/downloaddata) describes historical vintages and initial-release data, but shares FRED service terms. No ALFRED content is imported. Original-publisher release archives or a separately authorized vintage source are still needed for historical point-in-time evidence. A vintage date alone also needs a conservative, documented treatment of unknown clock time.

## Rights, access and update policy

Collectors use public linked files, TLS, sequential requests at least three seconds apart, robots checks and bounded timeouts. Denials, challenges, 429s and redirects stop collection rather than trigger evasion. No login, subscription or access control was bypassed. [EIA reuse policy](https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php) permits government content with acknowledgement but distinguishes protected third-party materials; retain dataset attribution and review commercial redistribution rights separately. EU and other publisher reuse rights are not inferred merely from HTTP 200. This stage performs local research and does not publish or resell source datasets.

H.10 XML itself announces retirement of the Board's customizable DDP download service during November 2026. The linked complete-release ZIP worked during this run; its continued availability is not guaranteed. Adapter failure is visible and a documented manual import is available. No fallback will silently switch to a provider with different terms.

## Economic interpretations: hypotheses only

- Refined product prices should be closer to gasoline import cost than crude prices, subject to product specifications, seasonality, freight and policy. This is an economic rationale to test, not proof of Lebanon's current coefficients.
- NYH gasoline USD/gallon × 42 minus Brent USD/barrel is a **cross-region gross price spread**. It omits yields, crude slate, operating costs, other products and regional basis; it is not measured refinery profit or a Mediterranean crack margin.
- Tax-excluded European retail prices retain distribution/retail components and potentially delayed pass-through. Converting units or currency cannot turn them into wholesale assessments.
- Fixed-lag historical correlations cannot identify the statutory averaging window, causal transmission, or forecast skill. See the report for actual sample counts and regime-separated exploratory results.
