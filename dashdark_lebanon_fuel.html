Plain-English Guide to Lebanon Fuel Prices

1. What does "2,445,000 LBP / 20L" mean?
In Lebanon, gasoline is priced per 20-liter tank ("tanakeh"). At 89,500 LBP per US dollar, 2,445,000 LBP equals $27.32 USD per 20 liters (122,250 LBP/L).

2. How accurate is the prediction model?
Across 69 historical decisions in the stabilized exchange-rate era, our top model (E_lightgbm) missed the actual 20-liter price by an average of just 8,801 LBP (~$0.10 USD per tank).
