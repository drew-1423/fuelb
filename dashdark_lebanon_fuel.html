Plain-English Guide to Lebanon Fuel Prices

1. What does "2,445,000 LBP / 20L" mean?
In Lebanon, gasoline is priced per 20-liter tank ("tanakeh"). At 89,500 LBP per US dollar, 2,445,000 LBP equals $27.32 USD per 20 liters (or 122,250 LBP per liter).

2. Why is there a "Station Quote" (2,391,000 LBP) and an "Official Price" (2,445,000 LBP)?
Fuel distributors (like IPT) often post the newest schedule on the day it comes out, while the Ministry of Energy and Water web archive can lag by several days. The dashboard shows both so you never get blindsided.

3. How accurate is the prediction model?
Across 69 historical decisions in the stabilized exchange-rate era, our top model (E_lightgbm) missed the actual 20-liter price by an average of just 8,801 LBP (~$0.10 USD per tank).

4. What is OVX (Oil Volatility)?
Think of OVX as the oil market's weather radar. A higher percentage means global traders expect bigger price swings over the next 30 days.
