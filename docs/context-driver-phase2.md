# Lebanon gasoline dashboard

## Users
Drivers checking a pump price and the conditional cost of their next fill-up, market analysts investigating the commodity signal, and reviewers checking evidence and model risk.

## Product
An existing Streamlit research terminal using stored Lebanese price evidence, market proxies and an immutable Hybrid forecast ledger. Driver Pulse presents a current eligible price and one next-update estimate; analyst and governance perspectives retain the full research tools.

## Constraints
Preserve purple/light-blue identity, all existing features, model weights, database schema and historical provenance. Only Hybrid is offered as the active model. Phase 1's strict integrity failure and insufficient live calibration remain visible. No accounts, purchases or external messages.

## Ethical stance
Uncertain prices are conditional estimates. Missing evidence is never filled with invented observations, times or confidence. An older quote never replaces a disputed newer record. Shared links retain the exact original saved prediction. Alerts are in-app and require no external destination.

## Success
Drivers can identify the latest eligible recorded price, exact forecast anchor, target, age, expiry and conditional fill-up difference without opening research views. Every existing feature remains reachable. At 375, 768 and 1440 px, controls remain readable and keyboard accessible. Exact forecast links resolve without live feeds. Bands require the saved horizon/artifact/level release proof and never bridge incompatible snapshots or unsupported targets.
