# Energy Market Research

Use this skill for the Lebanon gasoline forecasting project when establishing market drivers, the pricing mechanism, or the historical context for a move.

- Prefer primary sources for official price decisions, regulations, exchange rates, taxes, and government policy. Use authoritative market-data publishers for refined-product benchmarks, crude, freight, and related market series. Use reputable reporting to locate and corroborate decisions, not as a substitute for primary evidence when available.
- Verify each material claim against its source. Record the direct source link, publication or release timestamp and timezone, effective date (if different), retrieval date, and the exact series/product definition. State when an item could not be verified.
- Distinguish Mediterranean gasoline product benchmarks from crude benchmarks, and distinguish benchmark quotation currency, unit, delivery terms, and publication cadence. Do not treat a proxy as an equivalent series without explaining the limitation.
- Trace Lebanon's pump-price components and exchange-rate convention as they applied on each date. Verify historical changes to the mechanism, taxes/fees, subsidy or currency treatment, and the responsible decision-maker; do not apply today's rules retrospectively without evidence.
- Separate observed facts, calculations, and interpretation. Cite evidence next to claims and flag conflicting sources or uncertain effective dates.

Activate for domain research, sourcing market variables, or explaining price history. Do not activate for generic energy questions unrelated to this project's Lebanese gasoline forecast.
